Roadvanta Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Overview

Roadvanta ("the app") is an offline-style driving game. This policy explains what information is used when you play.

Information we collect

We do not ask you to create an account and we do not collect your name, email, phone number or exact location. Your game progress (coins, vehicles, settings, best scores) is saved only on your device and is removed when you uninstall the app.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device advertising ID, IP address and device details to show and measure ads, and to personalise them where allowed. See how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites . You can reset or turn off personalised ads in your Android settings (Settings > Google > Ads).

Internet and permissions

The app needs internet access only to load ads. It does not use your camera, microphone, contacts or location.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data sharing

We do not sell your data. Data is shared only with Google through its ad service as described above.

Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will always be available at the same link.
